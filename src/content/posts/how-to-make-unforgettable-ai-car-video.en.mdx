## What the Unforgettable AI car video is

The format people copy is a short beat-matched reveal:

1. Talk to the camera (often at sunset / open lot staging)
2. Raise two cards — **2** and **4** — reading **24** from the viewer’s left
3. Cards / hand cover the lens, then clear on the song drop
4. Car is parked behind you
5. Look back, point or react, walk the beat out

AI remakes keep that card flash, lens cover, and reveal timing from a **template video**, then recast the person in front — and sometimes the car — from photos you have permission to use.

Typical output format for social (this template):

- About **11 seconds** (other remakes run ~**12–15 seconds**)
- **16:9** landscape (1920×1080 source); crop or reframe in TikTok if you need **9:16**
- MP4, ready for TikTok / Reels / Shorts (many creators mute the file and attach the trend sound in-app)

## Before you upload: authorization comes first

Only use a **real-person photo when that person has authorized this use**.

- Prefer a photo of **yourself**, or someone who **explicitly allowed** you to generate and publish the video.
- The person must be **18+**.
- Do **not** upload celebrities, public figures, or anyone who did not consent.
- Do **not** use the result to impersonate someone or spread misleading content.
- For a custom car photo: use a car you own or have rights to show; cover plates if you need privacy.

Genjutsu AI expects you to confirm rights over every likeness you upload. If you cannot show permission, pick another reference — pets and illustrated characters are common alternatives when a person photo is not appropriate.

## Prompt and quality tips

**Do**

- Lock motion: same card display, lens cover, reveal timing, camera path
- Name assets: `@Video1`, `@Image1` (person), `@Image2` (car if any)
- Spell out **24 left to right** so cards do not read as 42
- Start at **480p** to check likeness, then rerun at **720p** / **1080p**

**Expect some AI limits**

- Hands and cards near the lens are hard — a cleaner source photo helps
- Face identity should match before and after the occlusion; if it drifts, retry with a sharper front-facing still
- Car paint/wheels only stay faithful if the car photo shows the whole vehicle in good light
- Add music in TikTok / Reels — keep the generate file silent or mute it on upload

If a job fails, retry with a cleaner front-facing photo and a shorter, clearer prompt. Check the on-screen credit estimate before each attempt.
